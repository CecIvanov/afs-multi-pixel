# Which merchants is v1 for, and in which languages?

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Tsvetan Ivanov
Map: [AFS Multi Pixel map](../map.md)

## Question

Who is the v1 target merchant: any multi-Market store, or a segment (CEE, Shopify Plus, agencies managing several stores)? What store sizes and event volumes should we design for? Consult `adfeedstudio:business-analyst` and `adfeedstudio:marketing`.

**Settled (the user, 2026-10-01):** v1 ships in English only (admin UI and listing). The open question is the merchant segment and the scale to design for.

## Answer

**No segment (2026-10-01).** v1 is for any Online Store merchant with two or more Markets, in English. No segment-specific features (Plus, B2B, agency multi-store). The one thing this ticket fed elsewhere, the event volume to design for, moves to the "Relay and CAPI reliability" fog on the map.

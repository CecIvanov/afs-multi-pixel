# Storefront Market detection relies on the deprecated Liquid `localization.market`

Storefront customer events carry no Market, so the theme app embed reads Liquid `localization.market.id` and passes it on in the `_mpx_market` cookie, and the Pixel Mapping is keyed by that numeric Market ID. Shopify lists `localization.market` among the deprecated single-market endpoints ("Market IDs are no longer stable identifiers"), but its migration guidance is paused, with no replacement and no removal date. We ship v1 on it anyway, because it returns the most specific Market the shopper is actually in, which is exactly what per-Market routing needs, and no alternative exists yet. We cover the instability by flagging Markets without a pixel as unmapped (for example, a new child Market) rather than by inheriting a parent's pixel, and we watch the Shopify changelog for the replacement.
